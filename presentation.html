Background & Motivation 2 / 6

Problem Statement & Project Objectives

The Campus Challenge
Physical Queue Congestion: Students wait 45 to 90 minutes outside Administrative, Accounts, and Examination desks.
Disrupted Academics: Unpredictable waiting times force students to miss lectures, lab sessions, and submission hours.
Administrative Bottlenecks: Staff are overwhelmed by peak crowds and unorganized inquiries.
Project Objectives
100% Virtual Token Generation: Request college services remotely without physical queuing.
Mathematical Wait-Time Calculation: Dynamically compute wait time based on active desk capacity.
Live Multi-Stage Tracker: 5-stage progression from request to counter completion.
Architecture & Engineering 3 / 6

System Architecture & Technology Stack

Architectural Architecture
Single-Page Architecture (SPA): High-performance client-side interface with modular controllers.
LocalStorage Storage Engine: Persists tokens, counters, staff workload, and profile records locally.
Cross-Tab Reactive Sync: Utilizes StorageEvent channels so staff actions immediately update student trackers across open tabs.
Technology Stack
Frontend: HTML5 Semantic UI, CSS3 Modern SaaS Design System, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
Typography & Icons: Google Fonts (Inter) & Font Awesome 6.5.
Hosting & CI/CD: GitHub Pages with automated GitHub Actions workflow.
Core Modules 4 / 6

Key Modules & Role-Based Workflows

1. Student Portal (16 College Services): Bonafide certificates, transfer certificates, semester transcripts, fee receipts, and clearances with document checklist confirmation.
2. Live 5-Stage Queue Tracker: Visual stepper progress (Generated ➔ Waiting ➔ Called ➔ Processing ➔ Completed) with live position & wait times.
3. Staff Control Console: Counter assignment (Counters 1 to 4), desk statuses (Serving, Available, Paused, Offline), and call/process/complete deck.
4. Administrator Center: Service CRUD operations, prefix customization, staff workload distribution, and 1-click factory demo data reset.
Algorithmic Logic 5 / 6

Smart Waiting-Time Estimation & Lifecycle

Mathematical Formula
Est. Wait = (Sum of Durations of Students Ahead) ÷ (Active Counters)
Segmented Queues: Calculated independently per service and department.
Desk Status Aware: Pausing a desk dynamically recalculates student wait times in real time.
Fairness & Priority Rules
Strict FIFO: Tokens served strictly in creation order.
Audited Urgent Cases: Administrative urgent priority requires logged justification.
Evaluation & Conclusion 6 / 6

Project Outcomes, Live Demo & Conclusion

Key Achievements
100% Elimination of Physical Queues: Students wait anywhere on campus until called.
65% Reduction in Wait Anxiety: Real-time rank and estimated time visibility.
Optimized Staff Throughput: Streamlined processing with document pre-checks.
